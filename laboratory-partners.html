Supported import formats

CSV, TSV, JSON, XML, and, where applicable to the testing method, FASTA and VCF. Laboratory-specific formats are handled through a dedicated adapter rather than assuming every platform outputs data the same way.

Quality control on import

Every import is checked for missing markers, invalid allele formats, duplicate markers, and sample mismatches before it becomes available for comparison. Failed checks are held for review, not silently passed through.

Role-based dashboards

Separate views and permissions for laboratory admins, technicians, analysts, and reviewers, so staff only see what their role requires.

Full audit trail

Sample transfers, data imports, analysis runs, and report generation are all logged with a timestamp and an authenticated user, and are tamper-resistant once recorded.
