ABOUT

Software for the part of DNA testing that happens after the laboratory.

A DNA relationship test has two halves. The first is scientific: a laboratory extracts, reads, and quality-checks genetic material using validated equipment. The second is operational: tracking who submitted what, comparing profiles correctly, applying the right statistical method, and producing a report someone can trust and verify. GeneMatch builds the second half.

We started GeneMatch after watching laboratories manage cases across spreadsheets, email threads, and paper chain-of-custody forms, with results explained in ways that overstated what the underlying science actually supported. The goal was a system that keeps every claim tied to a method, a dataset, and a named reviewer, so a report never says more than the data allows.

GeneMatch is not a laboratory and does not perform DNA testing. We partner with accredited laboratories who handle sample collection and genetic analysis; our platform manages everything from case creation through the final report.

What we build

  • Case and sample management
  • Laboratory data ingestion and validation
  • Profile comparison and statistical analysis
  • Verifiable report generation
  • Role-based access and audit logging

What we don't do

  • Collect or physically test biological samples
  • Read DNA from photographs or camera images
  • Declare a legal result without laboratory data
  • Let software override a qualified reviewer
