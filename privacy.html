1. What we collect

Account information you provide (name, email, phone, organization, role). Case and sample metadata (case ID, sample ID, collection details, chain-of-custody events). Genetic profile data transferred from an accredited laboratory. Consent records. Usage and audit logs, including timestamps and device information where relevant to security.

2. Why we collect it

To manage your case, import and quality-check laboratory data, run comparison and statistical analysis, generate and verify reports, secure your account, and meet applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. We do not use genetic data for marketing or sell it to third parties.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access uses role-based permissions and, for staff accounts, multi-factor authentication. See our security practices summarized in how it works.
