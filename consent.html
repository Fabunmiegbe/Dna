CONSENT INFORMATION

Consent, in plain terms

Genetic information is sensitive. Before any sample is processed, GeneMatch requires a documented consent record tied to a specific, named purpose. This page explains what that means in practice.

What consent covers

Each consent record is tied to one purpose, such as a specific parentage or relationship case. Consent for one purpose does not automatically extend to another. If a laboratory or requesting party wants to use your sample or profile for something beyond the original purpose, a new consent record is required.

What we record

Consent status, the version of the consent form presented, the date and time it was given, who provided it, the authorized purpose, and an electronic signature or another approved consent mechanism.

Consent for minors

Where a sample donor is a minor, consent is obtained from a parent or legal guardian as required by applicable law, and this is recorded alongside the donor's own information. [Confirm exact requirements for your jurisdiction with counsel.]

Withdrawing consent

You can request withdrawal of consent where your case's stage and applicable law allow it. Withdrawal is recorded with a date and status. Depending on the stage of your case, withdrawal may stop further processing but may not remove data already required for legal or laboratory-accreditation recordkeeping. Ask your case manager what withdrawal means for your specific case.

Questions
