From sample to report

Laboratory testing

Accredited equipment extracts and reads the sample, producing a genetic profile.

Secure transfer

The profile is imported into GeneMatch through an authenticated, validated channel.

Comparison & review

GeneMatch compares profiles and a qualified analyst reviews the statistical result.

Report
