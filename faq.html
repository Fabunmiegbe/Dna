FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can GeneMatch read DNA from a photo, blood sample, or fingerprint?

No. No camera, phone, or photograph can read DNA. Genetic profiles are produced only by accredited laboratories using validated testing, genotyping, or sequencing equipment. GeneMatch begins after that profile exists.

Is a GeneMatch report legally valid?

It depends on your jurisdiction and whether the case followed a legally recognized chain-of-custody process. GeneMatch does not automatically mark a result as legally valid; each case has a configurable legal or forensic review requirement set according to where the test is being used.

What does "consistent with" mean instead of "proves"?

Genetic relationship evidence is statistical. A result states what the observed data is consistent with, under a specified set of assumptions and a stated methodology, rather than claiming absolute certainty.

What happens if a sample fails quality control?

A failed quality check blocks the profile from being used in comparison or analysis until a laboratory reviewer resolves it. Results are never produced from data flagged as failing.

Who can see my genetic data?

Access is restricted by role. Only the laboratory staff and reviewers assigned to your case can view your profile, and every access is logged. See our privacy policy for detail on retention and sharing.

Can I withdraw consent after testing has started?

You can withdraw consent where applicable law and your case's stage allow it. See consent information for how withdrawal is recorded and what it affects.
